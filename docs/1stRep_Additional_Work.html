Major Systems Built (Not in Original Scope)

1. Complete EPOS Platform

3 separate EPOS systems for Vendor, Reseller, and Storefront sales - enabling in-person retail capability across all business channels.

2. Multi-tenant Storefront Marketplace

White-label storefronts for resellers with custom branding, product selection, and pricing control.

3. Stripe Connect Payout System

Direct bank transfers to resellers with automatic commission calculations and payout management.

4. Comprehensive Support Ticket System

Full customer service management with live chat, ticket tracking, and priority escalation.

5. Real-time WebSocket Infrastructure

Live updates across the platform for inventory, orders, notifications, and chat.

6. Reseller Licence & Own Products System

4-tier licensing allowing resellers to sell their own products on their storefronts with admin approval workflow.

7. Warehouse & Store Location Management

Complete WMS with multiple warehouse/store locations, stock transfers, and location-based inventory tracking.

8. Community & Athlete Program

Brand ambassador and sponsored athlete management with application system and profile showcases.

9. Show Reel & Media System

Video showcase functionality with media gallery management for brand storytelling.

10. Smart Notification System

Complete notification centre with real-time WebSocket notifications, categories, priorities, and multi-channel delivery.

11. Admin Content Controls

Homepage section management, pop-up messages, headlines, announcement banners, and dynamic product sections - all admin controlled.

12. Smart Inventory & Product Performance

Advanced inventory analytics with product performance tracking, reorder suggestions, and dead stock identification.

13. Receipt & Label Printing System

Digital receipts, EPOS printing, shipping labels, packing slips, and bulk label printing functionality.

14. Partner Analytics Dashboard

Comprehensive reseller/partner performance tracking, commission analytics, and comparison reports.

Payment Integrations Added

The original scope specified Stripe only. The delivered platform includes:

  • Square Web Payments - Complete integration as primary processor
  • Apple Pay - Native mobile payments
  • Google Pay - Native mobile payments
  • PayPal - Alternative payment method
  • QR Pay - Contactless EPOS payments
  • Card Reader - Physical card reader support
  • Stripe Connect - Reseller commission payouts

Conclusion

The delivered 1stRep platform significantly exceeds the original 12-page specification. What began as a standard e-commerce platform has evolved into a comprehensive B2B/B2C marketplace with:

  • Multiple payment processors instead of Stripe-only (Square, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, QR Pay)
  • Complete EPOS capability for in-person sales (3 separate systems)
  • Multi-tenant reseller storefronts for white-label selling
  • Reseller licence system with 4-tier product licensing
  • Reseller own products - licensed resellers can sell their own products
  • Automated commission payouts via Stripe Connect
  • Complete warehouse management with store locations and stock transfers
  • Community & Athlete program for brand ambassadors
  • Show reel & media system for video showcases
  • Smart notification system with real-time WebSocket alerts
  • Admin content controls for homepage, pop-ups, headlines, and sections
  • Smart inventory with product performance analytics
  • Receipt & label printing for all order types
  • Partner analytics dashboard for reseller performance tracking
  • 5-tier user system instead of 2 roles
  • Full support ticket system with live chat beyond basic chatbot
  • B2B access control with separate portals and workflows

The additional work represents approximately 5x the original feature set
and transforms the platform from a basic e-commerce site into a comprehensive business operating system.
