2. Platform Overview

Business Model

Your platform operates two distinct sales channels:

B2C (Direct to Consumer)

Customers browse your product catalogue, add items to cart, and checkout using card payments or PayPal. They can create accounts to track orders, save wishlists, and manage returns.

B2B (Business to Business)

Three partner types can sell or purchase your products:

  • Resellers - Run their own branded storefronts selling your products for commission
  • Wholesalers - Purchase bulk orders at discounted prices
  • Vendors - Operate point-of-sale terminals at physical locations

Target Market

  • UK-based fitness enthusiasts
  • Gym owners and personal trainers
  • Fitness apparel retailers
  • Outdoor and tactical gear enthusiasts

6. Payment Processing

All payments are securely processed through industry-leading providers. Your platform never stores card details - they're handled entirely by Square and PayPal.

Square (Primary)

Handles all card payments across the platform:

  • Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay
  • QR code payments

PayPal (Alternative)

Offers customers additional payment flexibility:

  • PayPal account payments
  • PayPal card processing
  • Pay Later options

Stripe Connect (Reseller Payouts)

Enables direct commission payments to resellers:

  • Resellers connect bank accounts via Stripe
  • Identity verification for security
  • Instant transfers to bank accounts

EPOS Payment Methods

Five payment options for in-person sales:

  1. Tap/Insert - Card reader for contactless or chip
  2. Card - Manual card entry
  3. Cash - Cash payments with change calculation
  4. QR Pay - Customer scans QR code to pay online
  5. PayPal - PayPal payment link

9. Support & Maintenance

Common Admin Tasks

Adding a New Product

  1. Go to Admin Dashboard > Products
  2. Click "Add Product"
  3. Fill in product details (name, description, price)
  4. Upload product images
  5. Add size/colour variants
  6. Set stock levels
  7. Save and publish

Processing an Order

  1. Go to Admin Dashboard > Orders
  2. Find the order
  3. Update status to "Processing"
  4. When shipped, add tracking number
  5. Update status to "Shipped"
  6. Customer receives shipping email automatically

Approving a Reseller

  1. Go to Admin Dashboard > Resellers
  2. View pending applications
  3. Review business details
  4. Click "Approve" or "Reject"
  5. Set commission rate and tier

Troubleshooting

Issue Solution
Payment not processing Check Square/PayPal dashboard for errors
Images not uploading Ensure images are under 5MB and in JPG/PNG format
Emails not sending Check Gmail API connection in settings
Site running slowly Contact hosting support for scaling
