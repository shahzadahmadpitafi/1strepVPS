Programme Overview

The 1stRep Influencer Programme is a performance-based partnership designed for athletes, coaches, and fitness creators. When your followers buy using your personal discount code, you earn flat credits per sale — which you can convert to cash or store credit at any time.

The Core Loop

  • You get a personal discount code (e.g. SMITH10) to share with your audience.
  • Every time someone uses that code at checkout, you earn a fixed number of credits instantly — no need to chase them.
  • Accumulate credits, then draw them down as cash (50p per credit) or exchange them for store credit at full value (£1 per credit).

How to Apply

Any athlete, coach, or fitness creator can apply via the public Influencer Programme page on the 1stRep website.

  1. Visit the application page

    Navigate to Influencer Programme → Apply Now from the main menu, or go directly to /influencer-login and click the Apply Now tab.

  2. Fill in your details

    You'll be asked for: full name, email address, phone number, sport/discipline, approximate follower count, and links to your Instagram, TikTok, and/or YouTube.

  3. Write a short message

    Tell the team why you'd be a great fit for the programme and how you'd promote 1stRep to your community.

  4. Submit your application

    You'll receive an automatic confirmation email. The 1stRep team will review your application and respond within a few business days.

✅ A confirmation email is sent to you immediately after submitting. The admin team is also notified automatically so nothing falls through the cracks.

Admin Approval Process

This section is for the 1stRep team. Here's what happens when an application is reviewed.

Reviewing an Application

All pending applications appear under Admin → Influencer Credits. Each card shows the applicant's social handles, follower count, and message.

  1. Review the applicant card

    Check their follower count, social links, and message to assess fit with the brand.

  2. Approve or Reject

    Click Approve to onboard them, or Reject to decline. Rejected applicants are notified automatically.

  3. What happens on approval (automatic)

    The system creates their user account, generates a unique tracking slug from their name, awards 100 welcome credits, creates two default discount codes (e.g. SMITH10 active, SMITH15 inactive), and emails them login credentials.

Discount Codes & Variants

You can hold multiple discount code variants but only one can be active at a time. The active code is what you share publicly.

How a code is constructed

Your code = [your tracking slug] + [suffix]

Example: tracking slug smith, suffix 10 → full code SMITH10

Variant fields

FieldDescription
Code SuffixThe part you choose, e.g. 10, 15, VIP, SUMMER
Customer Discount %How much off the customer gets at checkout (e.g. 10 = "10% off")
Fixed Credits Per SaleHow many credits land in your account each time the code is used (flat, regardless of order value)
ActiveOnly one variant is active at a time — this is the one to share
Use CountTotal number of times this code has been redeemed

Creating a new variant

  1. Go to the Codes tab

    Click New Code in the top right.

  2. Set your suffix

    Pick a memorable suffix. Your full code will be shown as you type — make sure it's easy to say aloud in a post.

  3. Choose the customer discount

    A higher discount drives more conversions but check with the 1stRep team about the maximum you're authorised to offer.

  4. Set credits per sale

    Enter the flat credits you'll earn each time the code is used. This is agreed with the 1stRep team — your default codes already have this pre-set.

⚠️ Only the active code earns you credits. If you switch to a new variant, make sure to update the code you share publicly. Credits stop accumulating on deactivated codes.

Switching active codes

In the Codes tab, click Set Active on any variant to make it your current public code. The previous active code is automatically deactivated.

Redeeming Credits

You can redeem your credits at any time from the Credits tab. Redemption requests go to the 1stRep team who review and fulfil them.

Redemption types

TypeValueHow it works
💰 Cash Draw-down 100 credits = £50 Cash paid directly to you (bank transfer or agreed method). Best for influencers who want liquid earnings.
🛍️ Store Credit 100 credits = £100 Applied to your 1stRep account to spend on any products. Best value — full £1 per credit.
👕 Clothing Credit Admin arranges Exchanged for 1stRep garments. The team will contact you to agree the items.
💡 Store credit gives you double the value of a cash draw-down. If you plan to buy 1stRep products, always choose store credit over cash.

How to submit a redemption request

  1. Go to Credits tab → Redeem

    Click the card for the redemption type you want (Cash Draw-down, Store Credit, or Clothing Credit).

  2. Choose the amount

    Enter the number of credits in multiples of 50. The cash/credit value is shown live so you know exactly what you'll receive.

  3. Add optional notes

    For cash draw-downs, you may want to include your preferred payment method or bank details in the notes. For clothing credits, describe the items you're interested in.

  4. Submit

    Your request is logged and the team is notified. Credits are held as pending until the request is approved or fulfilled.

Garment Drop

Selected influencers are enrolled in the Garment Drop programme, which gives them access to new product releases, samples, and exclusive pieces ahead of the general public.

Eligibility

Garment drop eligibility is set by the 1stRep admin team and is based on performance, audience engagement, and brand fit. There's no fixed threshold — the team selects influencers on a case-by-case basis.

ℹ️ If you've been made garment-drop eligible, you'll see a Garment Drop badge on your profile in the admin panel. The team will reach out directly to arrange fulfilment.

For admins

To mark or remove garment drop eligibility for an influencer, go to Admin → Influencer Credits, select the influencer, and click the Mark Garment Drop / Remove Garment Drop button in their profile card.

Tracking Links & Referrals

Every influencer has a unique tracking link — a personalised URL that records visits from your audience even if they don't use a discount code at checkout.

Your tracking link

Format: https://1strep.com/?ref=[your-slug]

Example: https://1strep.com/?ref=smith

When to use it

  • Bio link — put it in your Instagram/TikTok bio so all profile visits are tracked
  • Story swipe-up — use in story links on Instagram
  • YouTube description — place it at the top of every video description

What gets tracked

Code Use CountPer-variant use counts on the Codes tab

Total Sales GeneratedTotal order value driven by your codes (£)
💡 Combine your tracking link with your discount code for maximum impact: "Visit 1stRep at [link in bio] and use code SMITH10 for 10% off." The link captures the audience, the code drives the purchase.

Processing a redemption

  1. Go to the Redemptions tab

    All requests appear here sorted by date, newest first. Pending requests are highlighted.

  2. Click Review

    A dialog appears showing the influencer, redemption type, amount, and any notes they left.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do credits expire?

No. Credits never expire. They stay in your account until you choose to redeem them.

Can I have multiple active codes at the same time?

No — only one discount code variant can be active at a time. This is to keep your tracking clean. You can switch between variants at any point, but only the active one earns credits and gives customers a discount.

When do my credits appear after a sale?

Credits are awarded automatically and appear in your balance as soon as the order payment is confirmed — typically within seconds of checkout, whether the customer paid online, via EPOS, or via QR.

What's the minimum I can redeem?

50 credits (worth £25 cash or £50 store credit). Redemptions must be in multiples of 50.

How long does a redemption take to be fulfilled?

The team aims to process all redemption requests within 3–5 business days. You can track the status (Pending → Approved → Fulfilled) in your Credits tab.

Can I choose how I get my cash draw-down paid?

Yes — include your preferred payment method (e.g. bank transfer, PayPal) in the notes field when submitting the request. The team will confirm before processing.

What happens to my credits if I close my account?

Please contact the 1stRep team before closing your account. Any outstanding credits can be redeemed in advance of closure.

Can I create a code with a high customer discount (e.g. 30%)?

Custom discounts above the defaults need to be agreed with the 1stRep team first. The platform allows any value to be set, but please confirm with your account manager before promoting a custom discount level.

I submitted content — why haven't I received my credit?

Content credits are awarded when a post is approved by the admin team, not on submission. If your content has been pending for more than 48 hours, reach out to your account manager.
